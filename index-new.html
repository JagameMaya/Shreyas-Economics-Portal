📘 Guidance: how entries are scored & ranked

1. AI score out of 10

Each new entry is graded automatically by an AI examiner, judged by what the entry is trying to do. The call type sets the default, but the AI follows the clear intention of the text:

  • Forecast: takes a position on what will happen (Rate Cut / Hold / Hike, House Price Growth / Correction, Bubble, Panic Selling, Risk-Off).
  • Analysis: explains, critiques or evaluates (Policy Critique, Cognitive Bias, Wage-Inflation, Sentiment, Macro Trend, Sector Rotation, Credit Spread, Rental Market notes).

Five criteria, 0–2 marks each:

CriterionForecastAnalysis
Evidence & dataSpecific, dated, sourced figures (ONS, BoE, OBR, market pricing).
Economic reasoningA clear causal chain from the evidence to the conclusion.
3rdClear, testable call: direction, by when, how it will be judged.Clear thesis & conclusion: a central argument that is answered.
4thRisks & invalidation: the main risk, what would change your view, confidence. A one-sided, conviction call is fine.Evaluation & judgement: weighs factors and limitations, then reaches a justified view.
Clarity & structureConcise, well organised, correct terminology.

Scoring is calibrated to the life stage on the entry, so a Year 10 entry is judged against a strong Year 10 standard, not a professional one.

4. Integrity rules

  • The thesis (topic, call, date, rationale) is locked once committed. Only the outcome and post-mortem can be added later.
  • An entry can be deleted only within 1 hour of posting (to fix mistakes). After that it is permanent.
  • ⭐ Showcase: each author can feature up to 5 of their best entries at the top. Featuring only changes where an entry is shown, never its content or timestamps. Use 🔗 Copy link to share a specific entry, e.g. in a personal statement or CV.
  • Scores and points are written only by the server. Nobody can edit them from a browser.
  • Accuracy % counts resolved calls only. Honest "Incorrect (Learned)" post-mortems are valued: reflection improves future scores.
  • The AI is a study aid, not an authority. It can't check live data and may misjudge. Use its feedback to improve, not as gospel.
